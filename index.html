Classroom OS Manual & Help Guide

Welcome to your complete teacher reference manual. Find detailed explanations, tips, and instructions for all dashboard tools below.

🔍
🚀 Quick Start & Dashboard Overview

The Dashboard Overview is the homeroom control center of Classroom OS. It displays time-sensitive information for the current school day.

📅 Today's Schedule Timeline

Displays all subjects and rooms scheduled for today in chronological order. Clicking on the Go to Timetable button lets you manage the schedule blocks.

📋 Daily Memo Board

A persistent writing pad for quick notes, reminders, or observations. Any text typed in the memo board is automatically saved in real-time and will be here when you return.

🎂 Today's Celebrations & Birthdays

Displays special banners for student birthdays on the current date, as well as dynamic US Federal Holiday notifications.

Tip: The timeline will automatically display a coffee-cup empty state on weekends, indicating no scheduled sessions.
🏫 Class & Roster Management

Classroom OS supports managing multiple classes independently. Switching classes changes the loaded roster, seating arrangement, and workgroups.

🏫 Switching Classes

Use the drop-down select widget in the topbar header next to the title. Select any class to switch rosters instantly.

⚙️ Creating, Renaming, & Deleting Classes

  1. Click the Settings Gear (⚙️) next to the class selector in the topbar.
  2. Add Class: Type the name of the new class under "Create New Class" and click Add Class. It will switch automatically.
  3. Rename Class: Modify the text inputs next to the classes in the list. Changes are saved immediately.
  4. Delete Class: Click the trash icon next to a class. You cannot delete the class you are currently viewing, nor can you delete the last remaining class.
Roster Isolation: Students, grades, attendance history, and seating charts are isolated per-class, while lesson plans, tasks, and profile settings are shared globally.
📐 Seating Presets & Safety

The seating chart allows teachers to map students to physical classroom desk layouts.

📐 Desk Arrangement Presets

Select your arrangement from the Desk Arrangement dropdown in the card header. Choose from:

  • Traditional Grid (5x5): A standard 5x5 layout of 25 desks.
  • U-Shape / Horseshoe: Desks along the outer border, leaving the center open for presentations.
  • Collaborative Pods: Desks grouped in distinct 2x2 clusters of 4, separated by walking pathways.
  • Lecture Center Aisle: Desks arranged in left and right rows with a central walking aisle.

🛡️ Student Preservation (No Data Loss)

When you switch layouts, any student whose desk is deactivated (e.g. placed in a walkway center aisle) is automatically returned to the Unassigned Students selection pool. Their attendance records, behavior status, and grades are safely preserved.

🎲 Shuffle Seating

Click Shuffle Seating to randomly assign all students to desks. Shuffling respects your active preset, only placing students in active desk coordinates.

🚦 Clip Chart & Star System

Promote positive behavior and class engagement using interactive clip charts and reward points.

🚦 Behavior Clip Chart

Navigate to the **Behavior Clip Chart** tab inside the Student Roster. Click the up (▲) or down (▼) arrows next to a student's name to clip their clothespin to a different level. Sound effects play on transitions:

  • Clip Up (▲): Improves status. Reaching the top level (Outstanding / Purple) automatically awards +1 star and plays a sparkling chime.
  • Clip Down (▼): Demotes status. Reaching the bottom level (Parent Contact / Red) plays a low warning buzzer.

⚙️ Customizing Behavior Tiers

Click Customize Levels in the clip chart header. You can rename tiers, change emojis, add new color tiers, or drag the handle (☰) to reorder the hierarchy. Click Save Tiers to save or Reset to Defaults to restore standard settings.

⭐ Awarding Star Points

Click the ⭐ + capsule pill next to any student's name in the roster list or student details modal to award stars for excellent work. This plays a synthesized twinkle arpeggio sound.

📖 Lessons & Prep Checklist

Plan curriculum sessions and prepare classroom materials ahead of time.

📖 Lesson Plans

Click Create Lesson Plan in the Lesson tab. Enter topics, objectives, procedures, and assessment instructions. Selecting a lesson displays details in the inspector. Click Print in the inspector to open a beautifully styled portrait print sheet for lesson notebooks.

📦 Today's Materials Prep Checklist

Located on the Dashboard Overview page. This checklist gathers prep items in two ways:

  1. Auto-Generated: Scans lesson plans scheduled for today's subjects and extracts the materials list.
  2. Custom Manual Items: Type a material item in the input box and click Add (or press Enter) to manually add prep items.

Clicking the delete (×) button next to any item removes it from today's checklist. Prep items and checked status persist locally by date.

🔊 Classroom Tools (Timer, Noise Monitor, Caller)

Integrated tools to keep your classroom structured and focused.

🎲 Random Student Selector ("Cold Caller")

Click Call Student in the Roster header. Click Pick Student to start a slot-machine name wheel animation. It will select a present student at random. On selection, click Award Star to reward them, or click Skip to roll again.

🔊 Classroom Noise Level Monitor

Located in the Overview sidebar. Toggle the switch to access your microphone. Speak to test the level indicator bar. Use sliders to set the alert threshold and microphone sensitivity. If the room volume exceeds your threshold for over 1 second, the card flashes red and plays your selected chime profile.

⏱️ Activity Timer & Sound Profiles

Located in the left sidebar. Type custom minutes in the input or select presets (1m, 5m, 10m). Select an audio profile for the alarm chime:

  • Classic Chime: A pleasant, synthesized arpeggiated major chord.
  • Zen Gong: A deep, warm vibrating gong tone with harmonic decay.
  • Digital Beep: Rapid triple square-wave electronic warning pulses.
💾 Backup & Licensing

Protect, backup, and restore your Classroom OS database.

💾 Exporting JSON Data Backups

To export your data: click your profile badge at the bottom of the sidebar. In the profile modal, click Export Data. This downloads a .json backup file containing all classes, schedules, tasks, grades, and customizations.

📥 Importing Data Backups

To restore from a backup: open the profile modal, click Import Data, and select your previously saved JSON file. Classroom OS will load the backup and refresh the app automatically.

🔒 Software License Locks

Classroom OS is protected by copyright. If the copyright notice in the sidebar is altered, or if the app is hosted on an unlicensed domain, it will trigger a Software License Lock screen, halting all execution to protect intellectual property.
